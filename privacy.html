Prism Forge — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 12, 2026

The short version: The Prism Forge app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no internet connection required — or used.

Data the app collects

None. Prism Forge has no servers, makes no network requests, and contains no third-party SDKs. We never receive data from the app about you or your device.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress is saved locally on your device only, using Apple's standard app storage. This includes:

  • Completed levels and earned stars
  • Best scores and best daily challenge streaks
  • Unlocked themes and badges
  • Settings such as haptics and reduced motion

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it. If your device backs up apps (for example via iCloud device backup), that backup is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not by us — we have no access to it.

Support emails

If you email support, your message may include your email address, iPhone model, iOS version, and the issue details you choose to send. We use support emails only to read, reply to, and resolve support requests. Support emails are not generated by the app, are not used for advertising or analytics, and are not sold or shared.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to answer and resolve the request, meet legal obligations, and protect the service from abuse. You can request deletion of your support correspondence at any time by emailing the address below from the same email account. We will delete the identifiable copies we control unless retention is legally required.

Tracking

Prism Forge does not track you, does not request the App Tracking Transparency permission (it has nothing to ask for), and contains no advertising or analytics identifiers.

Children

Prism Forge is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Purchases

Prism Forge is a one-time paid download on the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never receive any payment or identity information.

Changes to this policy

If a future update ever changes this privacy model (for example, optional cloud sync of progress), this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will be updated to match.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email prismforge-support@calimanu.com. Only include details you are comfortable sharing.
